What Happens When You Click a Pen? #shorts

DESCRIPTION
Click. Click. Inside every retractable ballpoint is a tiny rotating cam that turns a fraction with each press, landing on a ledge to hold the tip out, or dropping into a channel to let the spring pull it home. Then down to the tip: a ball about 0.7 mm across, rolling thick oil-based ink onto the page, the idea László Bíró patented in 1938.
