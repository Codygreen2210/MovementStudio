To make the Moon lap the zodiac 254 times every 19 years, the builder needed one exact gear ratio. Sixty-four driving thirty-eight, forty-eight driving twenty-four, a hundred and twenty-seven driving thirty-two. Multiply them: 254/19. Exactly.
